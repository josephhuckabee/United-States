About the Golden State

California is the most populous U.S. state with diverse regions from beaches to mountains to deserts. It has a technology hub (Silicon Valley), a major film industry (Hollywood), and world‑class agriculture in the Central Valley.

External data source: U.S. Census Bureau.

Fun fact: California has both the highest and lowest points in the contiguous U.S. — Mount Whitney and Badwater Basin.
